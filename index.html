The Mechanics of Singing

Educational resources for the Northwest Sound Chorus and vocal enthusiasts

Welcome to Our Vocal Education Hub

This repository contains a collection of educational materials designed to help singers understand and improve their vocal technique. Developed for the Northwest Sound Chorus, these resources cover various aspects of singing from basic anatomy to advanced musical interpretation.

Whether you're a beginner looking to understand the fundamentals or an experienced singer seeking to refine your technique, you'll find valuable information and practical exercises to enhance your musical journey.

An interactive guide to vocal anatomy and technique. Explore the APROAR(TE) framework covering Audiation, Posture, Respiration, Onset, Articulators, and Resonance.

About Northwest Sound Chorus

The Northwest Sound Chorus is a dedicated group of singers passionate about barbershop harmony and vocal excellence. Based in the Pacific Northwest, we bring together singers of all skill levels to create beautiful music and build lasting friendships.

These educational materials represent our commitment to continuous improvement and sharing knowledge within the singing community. We believe that understanding the mechanics of singing is key to unlocking each singer's full potential.
