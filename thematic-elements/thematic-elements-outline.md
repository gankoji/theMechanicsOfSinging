# Thematic Elements of Music – NWS Rehearsal Outline

**Audience:** Northwest Sound Chorus (30–40 adult amateur singers)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes (30 min presentation + 30 min exercises)  
**Visuals:** Projector/slides, handouts, live demos  
**Reference:** [But How Should I Sing It? (PDF)](But%20How%20Should%20I%20Sing%20It.pdf)

---

## 1. Introduction (5 min)
- **Goal:** Connect every musical choice to one of four themes: Melody, Lyric, Harmony, Rhythm
- **Visual:** Chart showing the four pillars
- **Slides:** “But How Should I Sing It?” Slides 1–2

---

## 2. Thematic Deep Dives (20 min total, ≈ 5 min each)

### A. Melody
- What is melody? Why does it matter for interpretation?
- **Actionable tips:**
  - Draw phrase shapes/contours
  - Plan breath placement at peaks/turnarounds
  - *Demo:* Sing “O Canada” opening phrase (call & response)
- **Further reading:**  
  - [Wikipedia: Melody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melody)
  - Slides 3–4

### B. Lyric
- Words = story & emotional driver
- **Key technique:** Diction choices for nuance
  - Vowel emphasis chart (handout)
  - Consonant weight for clarity
- *Exercise:* Speak/sing a simple poem, vary diction for interpretation
- **Slides:** 5–6

### C. Harmony
- Chordal context shapes phrasing
- **Actionable tips:**
  - Listen to “neighbor” part, match vowel/timbre
  - Tune 3rds & 5ths (use drone in sectionals)
- *Demo:* Director drones I–IV–V–I, singers blend/tune
- **Further reading:**  
  - [Wikipedia: Harmony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmony)

### D. Rhythm
- Pulse, groove, and rubato
- **Actionable tips:**
  - Mark strong beats, hold ties
  - Subdivision tapping before singing
- *Demo:* Clap & sing a syncopated barbershop tag
- **Slides:** 7–8

---

## 3. Transition & Q&A (5 min)
- Recap key take-aways (one slide)
- Field 1–2 clarifying questions

---

## 4. Interactive Exercises (30 min)

| Time | Focus   | Format/Activity                                  |
|------|---------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 6 m  | Melody  | Echo director’s phrases                          |
| 10 m | Lyric   | *Small groups* mark/emphasize words in a poem    |
| 7 m  | Harmony | Sing 4-part I–IV–V–I, tune & blend over drone    |
| 7 m  | Rhythm  | Tap subdivision + sing syncopated tag (C&R)      |

> *Note: Small group lyric drill = max 10 min, everyone stays on risers.*

---

## 5. Ongoing Director/Music-Team Suggestions

- **Weekly 5 min warm-ups:**  
  - Vowel-matching on sustained chords  
  - Rhythm clapping pattern of the week  
- **Repertoire annotations:**  
  - Mark melody jumps & lyric keywords in scores  
- **“Minute drills” at end of rehearsal:**  
  - Quick echo-phrases (melody), one-word emphasis (lyrics)  
- **Recording & feedback:**  
  - Assign one member per theme to note observations each week
