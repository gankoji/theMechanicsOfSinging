# But, How Should I Sing It?!?

**Presented by Zach Groeblinghoff**

---

## Focus Areas
- Expressive singing
- Differentiating between the four Barbershop Harmony Society (BBS) themes
- Developing a process for analyzing lyrics
- Recognizing and expanding your vocal "paint palette"

## Why Study BBS Themes?
- To understand expressive singing, referencing artists like Crossroads and Stevie Wonder.

## Themes in BBS
- Melodic
- Lyric
- Rhythmic
- Harmonic
- (And how they can mix)

## Prosody
- Used to analyze prose and poetry for intonation and inflection.
- Helps identify important words in a phrase to better communicate intention or story.
- Understanding arrangement form allows shaping emphasis, developing an arc, climax, or pinnacle.

### Prosody Categories
- **Descriptors (Adjectives) [D]**
- **Conjunctions (and, if, then, etc.) [C]**
- **Terms of Endearment [ToE]**
- **Time-related words (Never, Always, etc.) [T]**
- **Repeated Words [R]**
- **Pivotal Words (Negatives) [P]**

## Vocal Paint Palette (Categories to Manipulate)
- Articulation (how you begin or end a pitch)
- Dynamics (volume)
- Rhythm (stressed/unstressed patterns)
- Tone/Timbre (dark/bright, breathy/brassy)
- Vowels (modification, dialect/accent)
- Consonants (emphasis and timing)
- Embellishments (scoops, neighbor tones, riffs, etc.)

## Examples
- **Quartets:** Smoke Ring (“Baby One More Time”), GQ (“Pity Party”), Midtown (“Never Give All the Heart”)
- **Soloist:** Barbra Streisand (“Cry Me a River”)

### Vocal Techniques
- Neighbor tone
- Fall offs
- Scoops & bends

- Accent
- Riffs
- Breathy

- Crescendo & decrescendo

- Growl

## Takeaways
- Focus on expressive singing
- Understand and differentiate BBS themes
- Develop a process for lyric analysis
- Expand your vocal paint palette
